PINK LIQUID GLASS ANNIVERSARY WEBSITE

1) Extract this ZIP.
2) Put your own audio files in the music folder with these exact names:
   - multo.mp3
   - about-you.mp3
   - the-world.mp3
   - what-up-people.mp3
3) Put your four photos in photos as photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg, photo4.jpg.
4) Open index.html in Chrome. Tap Enter & Play once to allow music playback.
5) Use the small cassette button at the top-right to open/close the player.

The player reads actual audio metadata: duration, current time, seeking and volume.
Audio files are not included; use audio files you are allowed to use.
